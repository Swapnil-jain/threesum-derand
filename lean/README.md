# Lean 4 kernel checks

`make lean` (from the repository root) fetches the Mathlib cache (`lake exe cache get`), builds `ThreesumLean/` and
prints the axioms of the main theorems. It needs Lean v4.34.1, installed through elan (the version is pinned in
`lean-toolchain`), and Mathlib v4.34.1 (pinned in `lake-manifest.json`). The build takes about 13 minutes on 3 cores.
Nothing here uses `native_decide` or `sorry`. `ThreesumLean/Axioms.lean` prints the axioms of the main theorems:
only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

These files check the arithmetic of the certificates and the integer lemmas. That each route really costs what its
terms say is a paper proof (`proofs/`), not checked here.

## What is Lean-checked

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `Basic.lean` | Rational bounds on `Real.log`, from Mathlib's `Real.abs_log_sub_add_sum_range_le` with `x = 2^k (1 - t)` and `log 2 = -log(1 - 1/2)`. The partial sums are computed in `ℚ` by `decide +kernel`; nothing analytic is assumed. Also the closed forms `H`, `gammaRho`, `gammaExact`, `qExp`, `Efull`, `Epruned`, written as in `certify.py`. |
| `Certs.lean` | One structure per script. Every check the script makes is a field, written in the script's form, and so is every printed saving and exponent. |
| `Points.lean` | Generated by `gen.py` from `specs.py`. Each positive theorem states the full structure at the script's exact rational point with the printed values. The `*_fails_*` theorems are negative controls, each proved as a negation. |
| `Finite.lean` | The integer lemmas of L1 and Appendix C, below. |
| `Stacked.lean` | Generated by `gen_stacked.py`. The folded all-edges Exact Triangle route (Theorem A', folded residue instances): `CertStacked` has one field per check of its certificate, `eps (E + gamma - 1/4) < 1` included. `stacked_T4`: `sigma >= 0.004057248470`, all-edges ET, (min,+), APSP `<= 2.995942752` and `O(n^2.995943)`; `stacked_T1` (paper lemmas): `sigma >= 0.003760515625`, `O(n^2.996240)`. Controls: next grid points of `s0`, `bA`, `oA`, theta moved by `10^-6`, and `eps E > 1` at both points (the unfolded instances do not apply there). |
| `KClique.lean` | Generated by `gen_stacked.py`. From the same sigma: deterministic Zero/Min/Max-Weight k-Clique `k - floor(k/3) sigma < k - 0.004057 floor(k/3)` for every `k >= 3` (`0.003760` for the paper lemmas), the printed exponents for k = 3..12, and Convolution-3SUM through Exact Triangle (`2 - sigma/2 <= 1.997972`, worse than 3SUM's 1.996001). Controls: the uniform form one unit higher and the k = 12 exponent one unit lower. |

Certificates, all at the exact rational points of the scripts and with the values they print:
- `derand_cert.py`, all three points (Theorem 1): `sigma >= s0`, every side condition (each term positive, encodings
  cheaper than boxes, dense instance building, per-slot bookkeeping), 3SUM `<= 2 - sigma`, all-edges Exact Triangle
  and APSP `<= 3 - sigma`, and the `O(n^e)` lines strictly: `derand_T4` gives `O(n^1.996001)` and `O(n^2.996001)`,
  `derand_T1` gives `O(n^1.996291)` and `O(n^2.996291)`.
- `certify_L1.py`, both points (Theorem 2): all seven cost terms, the side conditions including the Cor. tradeoff check
  `eps*gamma < 1 - eps*E`, `S >= 0.003677999 / 0.003431999`, the exponents and `O(n^1.99633)`, `O(n^2.99633)`,
  `O(n^1.99657)`, `O(n^2.99657)`. Also `L1_*_exact`: the exact savings `0.003678 / 0.003432`.
- `certify.py`, the six points of `params.json`, with every check the script makes, including the choice of p and the
  building condition.

Negative controls, each proved false in Lean: the saving at the next grid point (`s0 + 1` unit in the last digit), the
first exponent at the next grid point down, and one parameter moved by one unit in its last digit (`specs.py`). A
next-grid control is left out where the printed bound is not tight, because it still holds (for example L1's
`0.003431999`, whose exact value is `0.003432`).

`Finite.lean`, proved for all integers unless noted:
- Halving: `floor(floor(x/2^l)/2) = floor(x/2^(l+1))`; `floor(x/2) ∈ [x/2 - 1/2, x/2]`.
- Exact Triangle window: `S_l/2 - 3/2 <= S_{l+1} <= S_l/2`; `|S_l| <= 3 ⇒ S_{l+1} ∈ [-3, 3/2]`;
  `S_l ∈ [2S_{l+1}, 2S_{l+1} + 3]`, inside `[-6, 9]` when `|S_{l+1}| <= 3`. Negative control: `S_l/2 - 1` fails.
- 3SUM window (`x + y - z`): `[S_l/2 - 1, S_l/2 + 1/2]`, `[-5/2, 2]`, `[2S_{l+1} - 1, 2S_{l+1} + 2] ⊆ [-7, 8]`.
  Negative control: `[-6, 9]` fails at x = y = -2, z = 3.
- Base level `|x| < d ⇒ floor(x/d) ∈ {-1, 0}`, the sums `[-3, 0]` and `[-2, 1]`, scaling `4S = 0 ⇔ |4S| <= 3`,
  Fredman's identity, and the (min,+) step `S_l(piv) - min S_l ∈ {0..3}` over any finite block.
- The unique-witness test, for every bit width and every multiset.
- The Alon-Naor bias: `2^-v - 2^-(7c+1) > 0` for `v <= 7c`.
- Appendix C: the dyadic identity for all `v, t, tau >= 0` below `2^K`, and the full per-triple reduction (shift
  `tau = 2h - w_ab`, the 2K + 1 instances, `3h < 2^K`) for all weights, which covers `check_negtri.py`'s exhaustive and
  random cases. Negative control: the shift `2h - w_ab - 1` fails at `w = (0, 0, -1)`, `h = 1`. Also by
  `decide +kernel`: `check_identity` at B = 4, and a wrong identity (offset 2 only) rejected.

## Not in Lean (paper proofs)

- That each certificate's terms are the costs of its route: the reductions, the cost accounting of Theorems 1 and 2,
  the Alon-Naor witnesses and the corollaries' reductions (`proofs/`).
- The closed forms (gamma, q, E) as the exponents of the paper's counts (Appendix A). Lean checks them only as formulas.
- `certify_levers.py` (the Las Vegas remark and the comparison route) is not ported, nor is the (min,+)-convolution
  corollary line. The k-Clique and Convolution-3SUM lines of the folded route are checked as formulas
  (`KClique.lean`); their reductions (cor:kclique, Conv-3SUM to 3SUM) are paper proofs.
- For `Stacked.lean`: the folding lemma (Lemma 3.2': entries, positions, time), that the folded instances meet
  Theorem O's hypothesis under `eps (E + gamma - 1/4) < 1`, and that the other lemmas are unchanged by it.
- The paper's own theorems, which are assumed as published.

## Regenerating

`python lean/gen.py` (needs python-flint) rewrites `ThreesumLean/Points.lean` from `specs.py`, using the closed forms
of `certificates/certify.py`, and prints the controls it chose. arb is used only to choose the rational bounds; Lean
checks every one of them. `python lean/gen_stacked.py` rewrites `Stacked.lean` and `KClique.lean` the same way.
